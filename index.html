OUR SCHOOL

A place to learn.
A future to shape.

Parafield Gardens High School is a vocationally-focused school in northern Adelaide, supporting students to discover their strengths and build their futures.

Our curriculum offers a broad range of learning opportunities, including engineering, electro-technology, computing, woodwork, metalwork, photography and multimedia.
